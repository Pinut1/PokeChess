ADW_Sample scene: 

A simple game in the style of a classic retro turn based strategy  game. The goal is to capture the enemy Headquarters. 

Controls:
RED: Human Player
BLUE: AI Player

- Select a unit or building by clicking it.
- Clicking again will start the next action/ability. 
- To Capture buildings, use Soldiers and move to the building. The Capture action can only be used when standing on a building.
- Houses generate income, visible only in the player Bank property atm. 
- Once a player wins/looses then win screen will be presented.
